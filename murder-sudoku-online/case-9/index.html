How to play here

  • One suspect per row, one per column and one per room.
  • Nobody stands on furniture, except on seats such as chairs, sofas and beds.
  • Tap a suspect card, then a square. Tap a placed suspect to pick them up again.
  • With nobody chosen, tap an empty square to cross it out.
  • Tap a clue to see its squares on the plan; tap it again to mark it used.
