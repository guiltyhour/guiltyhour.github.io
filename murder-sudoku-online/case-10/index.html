How to play here

  • One suspect per row, one per column and one per room.
  • Nobody stands on furniture, except on seats such as chairs, sofas and beds.
  • Place: tap a suspect card, then a square, or drag the card onto the plan. Drag a pawn off the plan to take it back.
  • Notes: with a suspect chosen, mark where they might be.
  • Crosses: rule a square out for everyone, or only for the chosen suspect. Drag across to mark several.
  • Tap a clue to see its squares on the plan; tap it again to mark it used.
