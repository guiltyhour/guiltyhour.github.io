What is a murder sudoku?

A murder sudoku, also called a detective sudoku, is a logic puzzle where you place suspects instead of numbers. Once everyone is where they stood, you know who the killer is.

How it works

  • You get the floor plan of a place, a house, a train or a hotel, split into squares and rooms.
  • You get a list of suspects and a few written clues: "Nora was in the kitchen", "Ted was not in the same row as the piano".
  • You place every suspect on a square: one per row, one per column and one per room. Nobody stands on furniture, except sitting on a seat.
  • When the plan is full, the victim appears. Whoever was alone with them, in the same room, is guilty.

The logic is the same: rule out what cannot be until one option is left. The difference is that here every deduction tells part of a story.

One solution, no guessing

A good murder sudoku has exactly one solution and never makes you guess. In Guilty Hour every case is checked by a program before it ships: only one arrangement fits every clue, and you can reach it by reasoning. You do not even need to know where the body is: it appears at the end.

Is it the same as killer sudoku?

No. Killer sudoku is a number sudoku with sums: groups of squares whose total must match a figure. There are no suspects and no crime. A murder sudoku uses no numbers at all.
