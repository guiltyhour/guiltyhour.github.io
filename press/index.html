In one sentence

A mystery game with a new murder every day, the same one for everyone, solved with nothing but logic.

Description

Guilty Hour has two ways to play. In The Scene, a murder sudoku, you place the suspects on the floor plan of a house by following the clues, one per row, column and room, until you find who was alone with the victim. In The Interrogation you work out where each suspect was, what motive they had and which weapon they carried, and which of them did it.

Every day brings one case of each kind, the same for everyone, with its own streak and a result you can share without spoiling anything, and every three days of streak earns a hint. There is also a library of 300 free cases, with plans up to twelve by twelve in four levels: easy, medium, hard and extreme. Every case is checked by a program before it ships: it has exactly one solution and can be reached without guessing. The only advert is one the player chooses to watch in exchange for a hint.
