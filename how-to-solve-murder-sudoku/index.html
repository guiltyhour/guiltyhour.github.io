How to solve a murder sudoku, step by step

In The Scene you get the floor plan of a house, a few suspects and a handful of clues. You place everyone where they stood, and whoever ends up alone with the body is guilty. Here are the rules and a real case, solved all the way through.

The rules

  • Every suspect stands on one square of the plan. Plans run from five by five up to twelve by twelve.
  • One per row, one per column and one per room. With five suspects on a five-by-five plan, every row, every column and every room holds exactly one person.
  • Furniture takes up its square: nobody stands on it. Every object belongs in its room, and in the app you can tap one to see what it is.
  • The body is not on the plan until you have placed everyone. Its square counts like any other.
  • The killer is whoever was alone with the victim: the only person in the room with the body. You do not name them: once the plan is full, the button becomes Solve the case and the app checks the whole plan.

What the clues mean

  • "Was in the Sleeper Car" or "was not in the Mail Car" is about the whole room.
  • "Was in the same row as the lantern" is that row, not counting the furniture's own square.
  • "Was near the chest" means the squares next to it, but only inside its room: walls cut it off.
  • "Was somewhere in column D" is exactly that.

In the app, tap a clue and the plan lights up the squares it talks about.

A real case: the night express

The daily case from 21 September 2026, rated easy. The night express stops dead in a snowdrift, and by the time the lamps are lit the diplomat Karel is dead in his compartment.

The suspects:

  • Anton, the driver
  • Oskar, the waiter
  • Zora, the violinist
  • Clara, the journalist
  • Dmitri, the general

The clues:

  1. Dmitri was not in the same row as the lantern.
  2. Clara was in the same column as the table.
  3. Anton was in the same column as the chest.
  4. Dmitri was not in the same column as the desk.
  5. Clara was in the same row as the desk.
  6. Anton was not in the Mail Car.
  7. Zora was somewhere in row 3.

Step by step

  1. Start where two clues cross. Clara was in the same column as the table, on D1, and in the same row as the desk, on B4. Column D and row 4: Clara is on D4, in the Sleeper Car.
  2. Anton was in the same column as the chest, column C. In that column C3, C4 and C5 are Mail Car, where Anton was not, and C2 is the chest itself. Anton is on C1, in the Staff car.
  3. Zora was somewhere in row 3. C3 is in Anton's column, D3 is the suitcase and E3 is Sleeper Car, which already has Clara. That leaves A3 and B3.
  4. Dmitri was not in the same row as the lantern, which is in row 2. Rows 1, 3 and 4 are taken, so Dmitri is in row 5. He was not in the desk's column, B; C and D are taken, and E5 is Sleeper Car. Dmitri is on A5, in the Coal Tender.
  5. With column A taken, Zora is on B3, in the Mail Car.
  6. Only Oskar is left, with row 2 and column E: Oskar is on E2, in the Smoking car, the one room still free.

With everyone placed the body appears: the diplomat Karel, on E1, in the Smoking car. Only Oskar, the waiter, was there. Guilty.

Start from Zora's row 3 instead of Clara and the reasoning comes in a different order but lands in the same place. That happens a lot: a clue that is spare on one route is the key to another. When you are stuck, go back to the ones you have not used yet.

Without knowing where the body is

Every case is generated and checked to have exactly one solution without seeing the body. You never have to guess where the crime happened: placing everyone is enough. At the end the body appears and tells you who was with it.

Tips

  • Start with clues that say where someone was, not where they were not.
  • Count the gaps: if a row, a column or a room has only one square left for someone, it is theirs.
  • Take notes: pick a suspect and, with Notes, pencil their initial where they might be. Crosses rules a square out for everyone. Drag a finger to mark several at once.
  • When you place someone, the rest of their row, column and room is crossed out for you.
  • The daily gives you three free checks to see whether you are on track, and every three days of streak earns a free hint.
