How it works

The Scene

A murder sudoku. You get the floor plan of a house and a handful of suspects. Read the clues — “Nell was in the kitchen”, “Iris was not in the same row as the candle stand” — and place every suspect where they stood: one per row, one per column, one per room. When everyone is placed, the body appears. Work out who was with the victim and accuse.

The Interrogation

A classic logic grid. Match every suspect with a weapon, a room and a motive, rule out what cannot be, and name the killer.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Both daily cases and 280 library cases are free. Extra case files, hints, ranks and removing adverts are optional one-time purchases. There is no subscription.

Do I need to be good at sudoku?

No. If you can read “Nell was in the kitchen” and cross off what cannot be, you can solve a case. The first cases teach the rules as you go, and there are three difficulties.

Can a case have two answers?

No. Every case is verified by a program before it ships: exactly one arrangement fits every clue, and it can be reached without guessing.

What happens if I get stuck?

Three free checks a day tell you whether you are on track, and a free hint every day points at the next logical step instead of giving the answer away.

Is it the same case for everybody?

Yes. The daily case changes at midnight in your time zone, and everyone plays the same one that day, so you can compare results with friends.

What data does it collect?

Almost none. There are no accounts. See the privacy policy.
